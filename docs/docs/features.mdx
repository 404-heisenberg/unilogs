# Features

UniLogs is a digital logbook for university students. You decide what an
entry records, log your work in seconds, and see where your time goes. Every
feature below is live at **[unilogs.vercel.app](https://unilogs.vercel.app)**.

## Accounts and sign-in

- Sign up with email and password, and confirm the address with a one-time
  code sent by email.
- Or sign in with Google.
- Reset a forgotten password from a link sent by email.
- Delete your account and all of its data from **Settings**. This asks for your
  password first and cannot be undone.

## Projects with custom fields

A project is a subject, module or activity you want to keep a log of. Each one
defines its own fields, so a lab project and a reading log can record
different things.

- **Guided creation** in three steps: name and description, then fields, then
  a reminder frequency.
- **Start from a template** (Study log, Workout), reuse the fields of one of
  your existing projects, or start blank.
- **Field types:** text, number, duration (in hours), toggle (yes/no) and date.
- **Project workspace:** each project has its own page with its entries,
  summary stats and actions.
- **Archive** projects you've finished without losing them, or **delete**
  them along with every entry.

## Logging entries

- Every entry has a date, a title and a free-text body, plus the project's own
  fields.
- The body is written in **Markdown** with a live preview. Lists continue
  automatically when you press Enter.
- UniLogs remembers your last project and defaults the date to today, so a
  quick entry is a title and one field.
- Unsaved changes are protected: the editor warns you before you navigate
  away.
- Every edit is recorded in an **audit log**, so the logbook remains a
  trustworthy record of what you did and when.

## Search and filters

- Search across entry titles, bodies and project names.
- Filter by project, tag and date range.
- Entries are grouped by day on a timeline, with the time logged shown on each
  card.

## Dashboard and statistics

The dashboard is made of widgets you can show, hide, resize and reorder. The
layout is saved per user.

- **Summary:** total entries, tracked time and your current logging streak.
- **Activity:** a 12-week heatmap of the days you logged.
- **What's left:** open to-do fields, grouped into overdue, due this week and
  no due date, with one-click completion.
- **Recent entries** and **Continue logging** to jump back in.
- **Upcoming:** your next calendar events, each with a **Log** button.
- **Time by project**, **Logging frequency** and **Due & dormant** (projects
  that have gone quiet), available from the widget tray.

Each project also has its own statistics: entry count, tracked time, last
logged, entries this week, and per-field insights such as totals, averages and
trends.

## Google Calendar suggestions

This is the app's external API integration. Connect your Google Calendar in
**Settings**, and UniLogs offers your events as entry suggestions:

- **Accept** a suggestion to log it: pick the project, fill in its fields, save.
- **Reject** suggestions you don't want to log.
- Upcoming events appear on the dashboard with a one-click **Log** action.

UniLogs reads your **primary** calendar only. See
[Tech Stack → External APIs](/tech-stack#external-apis).

## Reminders and notifications

- Each project has a reminder frequency (for example, weekly), chosen during
  project creation.
- When a project goes quiet for longer than that, UniLogs adds a notification
  to the **bell** in the app and includes it in an email digest.
- Turn reminders off at any time in **Settings**.

## Export and share links

- **Export** a project's entries as **CSV** (for spreadsheets) or
  **Markdown** (for notes and reports).
- **Share** a read-only link to a project report, for example with a
  supervisor. The report shows the summary, field insights and entries, and
  can be downloaded as CSV or Markdown. Viewers don't need an account.
- Share links can be **revoked** at any time.

## Tags

- Add tags to entries to group work across projects, such as "exam prep" or
  "group work".
- Rename and delete tags in **Settings**.

## Mobile and install

- Every page adapts to phone screens, with a bottom navigation bar and a
  full-screen menu.
- UniLogs can be **installed** as an app (PWA) from supported browsers.
- Errors and confirmations appear as toast notifications instead of silently
  failing.
