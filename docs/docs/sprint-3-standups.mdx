# Sprint 3 — Standup Log

Standups are held over Discord voice. Each member covers what they did, what
they are doing next, and what is blocking them.

The minutes below were written up from the meeting screenshots and
cross-checked against the commit and pull request history. "Done" items are
what each person had merged by the meeting. "Doing" items are inferred from
what they merged next. **Team members: please confirm or correct your own

Sprint 2 client review and the round 1 user testing results (see
