## Methodology

Round 1 combines two things, so the results are both observed (what people
actually struggle with) and self-reported (how they rate the experience):

1. A short, task-based session with each tester, run over a screen-shared
   Discord call. One team member reads out the tasks and times them; another
   takes notes on where the tester hesitates, backtracks, or asks a question.
   Testers are not helped unless they are fully stuck — the point is to see
   where the product is unclear on its own.
2. A short feedback form (Google Forms — no account required to respond),
   filled in immediately after the session while it's fresh.

Testers are a mix of people outside the immediate dev team where possible
(classmates, friends) so the feedback isn't just the people who already know
how the product works.

### Task script

| #   | Task                                                                                         | What we're watching for                                                              |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Sign up for an account                                                                       | Friction in the signup form, validation errors, time to complete                     |
| 2   | Create a new project and add one custom field (e.g. a "duration" field)                      | Whether the custom-field concept is understood without explanation                   |
| 3   | Log an entry for that project                                                                | Whether the entry form is discoverable and the field they defined shows up correctly |
| 4   | Open the dashboard and find their total logged time / stats                                  | Whether the stats are easy to locate and interpret                                   |
| 5   | Add a tag to an entry                                                                        | Whether tagging is discoverable                                                      |
| 6   | Connect Google Calendar (if the tester has a Google account) and check for suggested entries | Whether the value of the integration is obvious                                      |
| 7   | Locate (without completing) the reset-password and delete-account flows                      | Whether account/security actions are easy to find                                    |

For each task, record: time to complete, whether they got stuck (and where),
and any direct quotes worth keeping.

### Feedback form questions

Copy these into a Google Form. Keep it to under 2 minutes to fill in so
testers actually finish it.

1. Name or pseudonym (optional — for our internal tracking only, never
   published)
2. On a scale of 1–5, how easy was it to sign up and get started?
3. On a scale of 1–5, how intuitive was creating a project and defining your
   own fields?
4. On a scale of 1–5, how clear were the statistics on your dashboard?
5. Did anything feel confusing or broken? (open text)
6. Is there a feature you wish existed, or wish worked differently? (open
   text)
7. On a scale of 1–5, how likely are you to use this instead of a paper
   logbook or notes app?
8. Any other comments? (open text)
